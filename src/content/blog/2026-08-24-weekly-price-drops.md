## What Buyers Should Know About Price Reductions

When evaluating price reductions, buyers should compare recent comparable sales and consider how the property’s condition and any needed repairs affect value. Always factor in inspection results, potential retrofit or maintenance costs, current financing terms and appraisal risk. Local demand and inventory levels can change how meaningful a reduction is, so weigh reductions against market comps, inspection findings and your financing options.

A price reduction can indicate seller motivation, but it can also mean that the original asking price was higher than the market supported.

The reduction itself is only one piece of the puzzle. Buyers should still review comparable sales, property condition, disclosures, inspection findings, financing terms and neighborhood demand before deciding how much value a price change actually creates.
