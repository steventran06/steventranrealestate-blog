## What Buyers Should Know About Price Reductions

When evaluating price reductions, buyers should compare recent comparable sales, not just the list history, and factor in the property’s condition and scope of any needed repairs. Permits and quality of renovations (including ADUs) matter for resale and financing. Always order a thorough inspection, understand current financing costs and terms, and consider local demand and inventory levels—those market forces influence how meaningful a reduction actually is.

A price reduction can indicate seller motivation, but it can also mean that the original asking price was higher than the market supported.

The reduction itself is only one piece of the puzzle. Buyers should still review comparable sales, property condition, disclosures, inspection findings, financing terms and neighborhood demand before deciding how much value a price change actually creates.
