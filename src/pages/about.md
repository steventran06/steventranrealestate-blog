## Real Estate Experience That Started With Investing

I'm Steven Tran, a licensed real estate broker with [Diverse Realty Group](https://steven.diverserg.com/) serving the **Portland Metro Area and Southwest Washington**.

My path into real estate started as an investor and property manager. I've purchased and managed multifamily and residential properties in both Oregon and Washington, with hands-on experience in rental management, renovations, evaluating properties and understanding the numbers behind a real estate investment.

That background influences how I work with clients today. Whether someone is buying their first home, relocating to the Portland area, moving into a larger home, purchasing new construction or evaluating an investment property, I take an **analytical approach to home buying**.

A home is more than a listing and a purchase price. I want my clients to understand the property, neighborhood, market, comparable sales, potential risks, long-term considerations and the trade-offs behind the decision they're making.

## Helping Buyers Make Smarter Real Estate Decisions

My goal isn't simply to help someone find a house. It's to give buyers enough information and context to make a decision they feel confident about.

That can mean comparing neighborhoods and suburbs, reviewing recent comparable sales, understanding builder incentives, evaluating inspection findings, looking at potential rental income or simply talking through whether a particular home makes sense for someone's long-term plans.

My background in **business marketing, internet marketing and software engineering** also shapes the way I approach real estate. I tend to look at problems systematically, research the available information and help clients separate useful data from noise.

For buyers relocating to the area, I've also created a **[free Portland Relocation Guide](https://steventranrealestate.com/#relocation)** with information about neighborhoods, suburbs, pricing and what to consider when deciding where to live.
